# Kyoto day trip

We take the early train from Osaka and arrive before the crowds. The plan below mixes English notes with the Japanese names we need to show at ticket counters and on menus.

## Morning

Start at Fushimi Inari, 伏見稲荷大社, and walk up through the gates as far as the Yotsutsuji crossing. The view over the city is worth the climb. Bring water, because the shops near the top close early on weekdays.

伏見稲荷大社は京都市伏見区にある神社で、全国に約三万社あるといわれる稲荷神社の総本宮です。千本鳥居と呼ばれる朱色の鳥居が山の上まで続いています。早朝は参拝者が少なく、静かに歩くことができます。

## Lunch

Nishiki Market, 錦市場, for a slow lunch. Try the tamagoyaki and the pickles. Most stalls take cards now, but carry some cash for the small ones.

錦市場は「京の台所」と呼ばれ、約四百メートルの通りに百三十軒以上の店が並んでいます。漬物、湯葉、だし巻き卵など、京都らしい食べ物を少しずつ味わうことができます。

## Afternoon

Walk along the Kamo River to Gion, 祇園, and finish at Yasaka Shrine before the train back. If it rains, swap Gion for the Kyoto National Museum, which is open until five.

## Things to remember

- IC card topped up for the train and the buses
- A small towel for the shrine water basins
- The hotel address written in Japanese: 大阪市中央区難波五丁目
